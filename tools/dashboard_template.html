BioKGSuite: results explorer

Seven quality dimensions and nineteen metrics for six biomedical knowledge graphs, and a prospective drug repurposing experiment that grounds three LLMs in their evidence. Every number on this page is read from the result tables in the repository; the manuscript figures are drawn from the same files.

Rankings do not transfer

A reversal is a pair of graphs whose order flips between two dimensions. Rates near 0.5 mean the two dimensions say unrelated things about the graphs; 0 means they are redundant.

Reversal rate between dimensions

Share of the 15 graph pairs that reverse, over all six graphs. Ties and missing scores excluded.

Sensitivity to near ties and to MATRIX

Concordance (Kendall's W)

W treats each metric as a judge ranking the graphs. P is from 20,000 permutations of graph labels within each metric.

Rank dispersion across the discriminating metrics

Best and worst rank each graph attains across the metrics whose range exceeds 0.05. Every graph spans at least four of the six positions.

Grounding experiment

128 drug and disease pairs approved between 2023 and 2026, after every graph was built. Each query shows a disease and eight anonymised candidate drugs; arms differ only in the KG evidence attached. The lift is the gain in mean reciprocal rank over the no-KG arm.
